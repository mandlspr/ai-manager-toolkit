## Principle

For creative content the decision rule is the asset's lifespan. A durable brand asset — a main logo, a universal intro meant to last years — is bought, or produced in a hybrid setup. Ephemeral social content, alive for a couple of days, is generated. Cost and turnaround follow that decision rather than driving it.

## Why it matters

The gap is wide enough to invite the wrong call. A 30-second clip from a freelancer runs 1,500 to 10,000 €, over two to six weeks; generated video costs 0 to 15 € a month and turns around in minutes. Reading only those numbers pushes everything toward Make — which is how a brand's most permanent asset ends up produced under a free tier's licence.

## How to apply it

- Decide on lifespan first, then check cost and delay against that decision.
- Check rights and watermarking before generating: free tiers forbid commercial use and stamp their output.
- Check where the generation service runs when personal data is involved.
- Keep the hybrid option open — buy the durable assets, generate the daily ones.

## Point of caution

Generated video still fails on narrative coherence beyond about thirty seconds, on lip and voice sync, and on complex storytelling.
