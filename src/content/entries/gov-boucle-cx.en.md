## Principle

Customer experience is a governance instrument, not only a function affected by governance. The loop runs: AI system → customer experience → customer signal → incident → root cause → remediation → governance update → back to the AI system. A customer signal is an input to the rule set, not just a ticket to close.

## Why it matters

Risk sees the controls. Data sees the model. CX and CSM see what the AI actually does to the customer. A control that passes review and a model that passes evaluation can still produce an outcome nobody planned, and the first place that becomes visible is in customer signals. Without the return leg — remediation feeding a governance update — the same incident recurs under a new ticket number.

## How to apply it

- Route customer signals to root cause, not only to resolution.
- Close the loop explicitly: every root cause either updates a governance rule or is recorded as deliberately not updating one.
- Measure on customer outcome — did the customer achieve what they came for — rather than on whether the AI answered correctly.
- Treat voice of customer, complaints, CSAT/CES and escalations as governance telemetry from launch onward.
